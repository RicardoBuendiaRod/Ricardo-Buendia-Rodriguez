About me

I am Ricardo Buendia Rodriguez, a biomedical engineering graduate student at Texas A&M University. I began my M.S. in August 2026 and expect to finish in May 2028. At the Bagnato Biophotonics Laboratory, I develop dissolvable polymeric microneedles as a drug delivery method for photodynamic therapy against skin cancer, carrying each design from formulation and in vitro characterization through porcine and murine studies.

Before graduate school, I studied photodynamic therapy dose-response in melanoma models and spent a summer at the USP São Carlos Institute of Physics in Brazil investigating combined radiotherapy and photodynamic therapy.
